    - Implement `parseSearch(input: string): SearchNode` as a simple recursive descent:
      - Tokenize by splitting on whitespace, respecting double-quoted phrases
      - Recognize keywords: AND, OR, NOT (case-sensitive per OData spec)
      - Implicit AND between adjacent terms (when no explicit operator)
